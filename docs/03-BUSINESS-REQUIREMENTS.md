# 1. Executive Summary

ClientPilot is an AI-powered Client Intake Platform developed by Lewis Systems.

Its purpose is to help professional service firms automate the process of qualifying, routing, and onboarding new clients. By reducing repetitive administrative work and improving response times, ClientPilot enables businesses to focus on delivering high-value professional services.

The first release of ClientPilot is designed specifically for small accounting firms in South Africa.

---

# 2. Business Opportunity

Many small accounting firms rely on manual processes to manage incoming client enquiries.

These firms often experience:

- Time lost qualifying unsuitable leads
- Slow responses to potential clients
- Missed business opportunities
- Administrative overload
- Inconsistent client experiences

ClientPilot addresses these challenges by automating the client intake process using artificial intelligence and workflow automation.

---

# 3. Customer Problem

Accounting firms receive enquiries from businesses and individuals with different needs and budgets.

Staff members must manually determine:

- Whether the prospect is a suitable client
- Which services they require
- Whether they meet the firm's ideal client profile
- Whether a consultation should be scheduled

This manual process consumes valuable time that could instead be spent serving existing clients.

# 5. Business Objectives

ClientPilot aims to:

- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Improve response times to enquiries.
- Increase the number of qualified consultations.
- Improve client experience.
- Standardise client qualification.
- Increase operational efficiency.
- Enable firms to scale without hiring additional administrative staff.

---

# 6. Value Proposition

ClientPilot helps professional service firms:

- Respond faster to enquiries.
- Automatically qualify leads.
- Reduce time spent on unsuitable prospects.
- Improve internal communication.
- Book consultations automatically.
- Prepare staff before meetings using AI-generated summaries.

The result is a more efficient client intake process and a better experience for both staff and prospective clients.

# 9. Business Risks

Potential risks include:

- Resistance to adopting AI.
- Changes in third-party platforms.
- Poor quality client information.
- Dependence on external APIs.
- Small firms with limited technology adoption.
- Competition from larger CRM platforms.

Mitigation strategies include:

- Keeping workflows simple.
- Building modular integrations.
- Maintaining excellent documentation.
- Providing clear onboarding.

---

# 10. Assumptions

This project assumes:

- Accounting firms want to reduce manual work.
- Firms are willing to adopt automation if implementation is simple.
- AI can improve qualification accuracy.
- Faster responses improve conversion rates.
- Businesses value better client experiences.
