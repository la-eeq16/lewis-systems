# 1. Product Overview

ClientPilot is an AI-powered Client Intake and Lead Qualification Platform designed for professional service firms.

The platform automates the entire client qualification process—from initial enquiry to consultation booking—allowing firms to spend less time qualifying leads and more time serving ideal clients.

The first version targets small accounting firms in South Africa.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many accounting firms waste valuable time responding to enquiries from prospects who are not suitable clients.

Common challenges include:

- Low-quality leads
- Manual qualification
- Slow response times
- Lost opportunities
- Poor lead tracking
- Administrative overload

ClientPilot solves these problems by automatically qualifying prospects using business rules and AI.

---

# 3. Product Vision
