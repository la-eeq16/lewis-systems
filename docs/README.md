## About

Lewis Systems is a software and automation company focused on helping small and growing businesses streamline their operations through intelligent business systems.

Our first flagship product is **ClientPilot**, an AI-powered client intake and lead qualification platform designed for professional service businesses.

---

## Mission

Build reliable systems that solve real business problems.

---

## Vision

To become South Africa's most trusted provider of intelligent business systems for small and medium-sized professional service firms.

---

## Current Product

### ClientPilot

ClientPilot automates the journey from a prospective client's first enquiry to a qualified consultation booking.

Current development includes:

- Lead Capture
- Lead Qualification
- AI Lead Summaries
- Airtable CRM
- Slack Notifications
- Calendly Scheduling
- Gmail Integration
- n8n Workflows
