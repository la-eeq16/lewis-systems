# ADR-001: Company Name

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-30

## Decision

The company will operate under the name **Lewis Systems**.

## Rationale

The name is professional, scalable, and technology-agnostic. It allows the business to expand beyond automation into software, consulting, and AI solutions without requiring a rebrand.

## Consequences

- Professional brand identity
- Flexible future product range
- Strong personal connection to the founder
